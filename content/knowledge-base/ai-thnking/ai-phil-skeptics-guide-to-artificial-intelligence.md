"If home security is about making sure our possessions are safe, financial security is about keeping our money safe, national security is about keeping our country safe, then epistemic security is about keeping our knowledge safe." - Elizabeth Seger

* * *

**Questions of epistemic security:**

Who decides what information is "reliable" when verification becomes harder than fabrication?

What happens to democratic decision-making when we can no longer agree on basic facts?

How do we maintain a shared reality when AI systems can easily generate convincing falsehoods?

"I would put all projects that propose to substitute a computer system for a human function that involves interpersonal respect, understanding, and love in the same category...an attack on life itself." - Joseph Weizenbaum

* * *

**Questions of human condition:**

What happens to our relationships when we find AI systems easier to interact with than real people?

What do we stop questioning if we believe that AI "understands" us?

What happens to our collective decision-making when we defer to AI systems that "know us" better than we know ourselves?

"Computer languages do not contain terms such as happiness...\[thus\] Identifying and codifying our own final goals is difficult because human goal representations are complex." - Nick Bostrom

* * *

**Questions of alignment:**

How can we make sure AI systems respect human values (e.g., fairness) when values often conflict with each other and depend on the situation?

Who decides which human values AI should follow, and how do we handle disagreements between different cultures and groups?

When society's values shift, who has the authority to update AI systems, and how do we ensure changes actually reflect what most people want?
